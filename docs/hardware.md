# Hardware

## Current Prototype

- Raspberry Pi 5
- Raspberry Pi Camera
- ESP32
- microSD card
- Monitor
- Keyboard and mouse
- Wi-Fi connection

## Planned Final Hardware

- Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W or similar compact computer
- ESP32
- Small LCD display
- Physical calculator keypad
- Rechargeable battery
- USB-C charging circuit
- Custom calculator enclosure

## Hardware Roles

### Raspberry Pi
Runs the calculator interface, engineering tools, AI integration, and camera processing.

### ESP32
Planned to handle the physical keypad and send button presses to the Raspberry Pi.

### Camera
Captures handwritten or printed engineering problems for AI analysis.

### Display
A small LCD will eventually replace the desktop monitor.
